Three terms you can revisit anytime
Project
A workspace for one product's analytics. Its ID scopes events, people, and configuration so another project's records stay separate.
Person
An analytics identity, such as the visitor who generated events. One person can have several distinct IDs. A person is different from the account you use to manage Pulse.
QueuedEvent
A durable row holding accepted work until the worker processes it. Queued does not yet mean a processed AnalyticsEvent exists.
